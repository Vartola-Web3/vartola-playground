# Finance partner discussion template

> **Non-binding.** Subject to legal review. Subject to the regulatory structure being confirmed. No commitment to fund or to provide services. No regulated activity has been initiated. Vartola operates a Working Alpha on Stellar Testnet only, with no real money.

## Purpose

Structures an exploratory conversation with a bank, lender or capital provider about a possible future role. It is an agenda, not an offer.

## Topics

- Regulatory route and who holds which licence
- Source of capital and investor eligibility
- Custody and client-money arrangements
- Settlement asset and payment rail
- Reporting, audit and servicing expectations
- Data sharing and verification

## Vartola can show

- Proof Center with real Testnet records
- Risk and expected-loss framework (internal estimates)
- Reconciliation and operations health
- Security, audit-readiness and continuity documents

## Not discussed as agreed

- No commitment to fund or to accept capital
- No regulated activity is being carried on

## Signatures

Only if the parties later agree a binding document, drafted by counsel. This template creates no obligation.
