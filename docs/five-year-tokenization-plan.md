# Later scale hypotheses

The working Alpha exists in October 2026. Mainnet has no date. The years below are management hypotheses, not a statement that the product starts in 2027, and not commitments. Regulatory classification must be confirmed with UAE counsel.

## Funding strategy

Vartola should separate **company capital** (team, product, compliance, sales) from **asset funding capital** held in ring-fenced facilities or SPVs. Equity must not be used as an informal substitute for safeguarded investor or borrower money.

| Year | Company capital target | Asset capital target | Primary outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2027 | Grant work on the existing Alpha | No live-money target is set | Testnet rail, audits, and regulatory design |
| 2028 | $1.5m seed | $2m warehouse / SPV capacity | UAE logistics launch through an authorised structure; 20–40 financed assets |
| 2029 | $4–5m Series A | $15m institutional facility capacity | Multi-emirate scale, equipment finance, automated servicing and recovery |
| 2030 | $8–10m growth round | $50m cumulative funding capacity | GCC preparation, regulated transfer pilot if permitted, bank and originator integrations |
| 2031 | Strategic round based on unit economics | $150m cumulative platform capacity | Regional productive assets and a separately authorised real-estate tokenization pilot |

The first $150k target matches the maximum Stellar Community Fund Build Award published by the Stellar Development Foundation. It should fund a nine-to-twelve-month evidence program, not a regulated public launch.

## 2027 — Evidence and regulatory design

- Apply for up to $150k in ecosystem/grant funding.
- Allocate approximately: 35% product and ledger; 20% security and smart-contract assurance; 20% UAE legal/regulatory design; 15% partner sandboxes and compliance; 10% pilot operations and grant reporting.
- Decide the regulatory perimeter: finance/lease origination, arranging, private financing platform, custody, payment services, virtual-asset issuance, or a licensed-partner structure.
- Complete Sumsub and on/off-ramp sandbox certification, client-money diagrams, reconciliation, key governance, security testing, and three months of stable pilot telemetry.
- Exit gate: written legal perimeter, regulator/partner route, tested controls, signed pilot partners, and zero unresolved critical security findings.

## 2028 — Regulated logistics pilot

- Raise a $1.5m seed round with eighteen months of company runway.
- Secure up to $2m of separately documented asset funding capacity; do not describe this as company revenue or equity.
- Launch only under permission or a regulated partner arrangement.
- Target 20–40 motorcycles, vans, and light commercial vehicles with strict obligor and asset concentration limits.
- Publish audited facility reporting: originations, outstanding principal, arrears, recoveries, investor principal returned, income, platform fees, and reconciliation breaks.

## 2029 — UAE scale and equipment

- Raise $4–5m Series A after demonstrating repayment performance and repeat SME demand.
- Grow institutional asset capacity toward $15m.
- Add business equipment only where title, valuation, insurance, recovery, and useful-life controls are proven.
- Establish independent risk, compliance, and information-security functions; formal board risk committee and portfolio limits.

## 2030 — Infrastructure and regional readiness

- Raise $8–10m growth capital only if contribution margin, loss performance, and servicing reliability support it.
- Target $50m cumulative platform funding capacity through banks, private-credit funds, family offices, or regulated funds/SPVs.
- Pilot transfers or liquidity only if specifically permitted; never promise liquidity before a venue and rulebook exist.
- Prepare one additional GCC jurisdiction through a fresh legal and regulatory assessment rather than passporting assumptions.

## 2031 — Real estate as a separate regulated product

- Target $150m cumulative platform capacity, subject to performance and partner appetite.
- Launch at most one institutional real-estate pilot using a separate SPV, property title and valuation, audited cash flows, offering document, servicing, custody, and investor-eligibility process.
- Do not reuse a vehicle-finance token structure for real estate without a new legal classification and approval.
- Measure success by assets serviced, repayment quality, recoveries, investor net return, SME productivity, and reconciled on-chain evidence—not token price.

## Regulatory budget rule

The plan reserves meaningful operating capital for legal, licensing, compliance, security, audit, insurance, and annual supervision. VARA's published schedule lists, for example, AED 100,000 application and AED 200,000 annual supervision fees for a single Category 1 issuance, broker-dealer, custody, exchange, lending/borrowing, or management activity; multiple activities and professional costs increase the total. This is why $150k is sufficient for evidence and preparation, but generally insufficient for a standalone licensed launch plus runway.
