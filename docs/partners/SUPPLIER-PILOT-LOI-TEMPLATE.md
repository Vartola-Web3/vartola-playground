# Supplier pilot letter of intent (template)

> **Non-binding.** Subject to legal review. Subject to the regulatory structure being confirmed. No commitment to fund or to provide services. No regulated activity has been initiated. Vartola operates a Working Alpha on Stellar Testnet only, with no real money.

## Purpose

Records a supplier's interest in taking part in a Testnet pilot as an approved supplier that provides quotations, invoices, asset allocation and delivery evidence.

## What the supplier states

- Legal name, licence and contact
- Asset brands and types, locations served
- Willingness to provide quotations, invoices, VIN or serial allocation, delivery evidence, warranty and service terms

## Pilot roles

- The supplier cannot approve its own payment release, change facility terms, or bypass underwriting or compliance
- Payment on a real facility would go only to the approved supplier payout account, never to the SME

## What it is not

- No purchase order and no payment commitment
- No exclusivity or volume guarantee

## Signatures

Only if the parties later agree a binding document, drafted by counsel. This template creates no obligation.
