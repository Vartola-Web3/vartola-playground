# Insurance partner discussion template

> **Non-binding.** Subject to legal review. Subject to the regulatory structure being confirmed. No commitment to fund or to provide services. No regulated activity has been initiated. Vartola operates a Working Alpha on Stellar Testnet only, with no real money.

## Purpose

Explores whether an insurer or broker could cover financed assets and how claims would flow through the platform.

## Topics

- Cover for vehicles and equipment: comprehensive, theft, total loss
- Policy data needed: insurer, policy reference, expiry, deductible
- Claim workflow: report, file, assess, proceeds, facility impact
- Beneficiary or loss-payee arrangements
- Data exchange and verification of insurance status

## Vartola today

- Tracks insurance status and expiry per asset and shows it in the asset health indicator
- Records claims manually; allocation of proceeds is not automated until facility terms permit it
