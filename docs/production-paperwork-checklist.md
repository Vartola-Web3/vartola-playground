# Vartola Production Paperwork Checklist

This is the document set required before moving from a no-value prototype to a controlled real-money pilot. Final ownership and form must be confirmed with qualified UAE counsel and the applicable regulator or regulated partner.

## 1. Corporate and regulatory

- Corporate structure, ownership, directors, UBO register, and governance authorities.
- Regulatory perimeter memo covering finance, crowdfunding, arranging, custody, payments, virtual assets, RWA issuance, and secondary transfer.
- Jurisdiction/regulator decision and licence or regulated-partner strategy.
- Regulatory business plan, financial projections, capital, and insurance evidence.
- Outsourcing register and agreements for KYC, payments, wallets/custody, cloud, blockchain, and collections.

## 2. Customer documents

- Platform terms and electronic-signature consent.
- Investor agreement, eligibility, appropriateness assessment, and risk acknowledgement.
- Opportunity disclosure sheet with obligor, asset, economics, waterfall, evidence, conflicts, and risks.
- SME finance/lease agreement, security, guarantee if applicable, payment mandate, and conditions precedent.
- Fee schedule, annualised cost, tax, late-payment, and early-settlement disclosures.
- Wallet, custody, stablecoin, conversion, and on/off-ramp terms.
- Privacy, cookies, marketing consent, data sharing, and cross-border transfer notices.
- Complaints, conflicts, default/recovery, and wind-down disclosures.

## 3. KYC/KYB and financial crime

- Customer acceptance and risk-rating policy.
- KYC/KYB, UBO, sanctions, PEP, adverse-media, source-of-funds, and source-of-wealth procedures.
- Transaction-monitoring scenarios and alert guide.
- Enhanced due diligence, suspicious-activity escalation, and record retention.
- Sumsub data-processing, security, webhook, manual-review, and continuity assessment.

## 4. Client money, wallet, and settlement

- End-to-end money-flow diagram covering AED, stablecoin, wallets, fees, refunds, and failures.
- Safeguarding/segregation agreement with the regulated provider or bank.
- Daily reconciliation across provider, bank, internal ledger, custody wallet, and blockchain.
- Wallet ownership and custody model; key ceremony, signers, recovery, rotation, and emergency freeze.
- Transak/Circle onboarding, supported corridor, limits, pricing, refunds, chargebacks, and failure procedures.
- Stablecoin reserve/redemption due diligence, depeg response, and alternative settlement plan.

## 5. Asset and facility evidence

- SME licence, constitutional documents, UBOs, statements, accounts, financials, and tax evidence.
- Supplier invoice, asset specification, serial/VIN, valuation, inspection, title/registration, acceptance, and insurance.
- Credit memo, affordability, risk grade, amount/term/rate, exceptions, and approvals.
- Security/perfection evidence, tracking, maintenance obligations, and recovery plan.
- Schedule, investor allocation, disbursement proof, servicing history, and closure evidence.

## 6. Technology and operations

- Architecture, data inventory, threat model, access matrix, and environment separation.
- Secure development, dependency scanning, penetration test, smart-contract review, and remediation.
- Incident response, breach notification, disaster recovery, backups, continuity, and tested runbooks.
- Ledger specification, idempotency, maker-checker, exception queues, reconciliation, and audit export.
- Support, complaints, arrears, collections, hardship, default, recovery, and investor communications.
- Operational readiness test with named owners, evidence, defects, and sign-off.

## Launch rule

The Simulation/Stellar Testnet switch is a technical test control only. It must never enable production mainnet or real customer funds. Production requires separate configuration and formal legal, compliance, finance, security, and executive sign-off.
