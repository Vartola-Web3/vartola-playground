# Claude prompt — Vartola professional infographic video

Copy everything below into Claude, then upload all six PNG files from `docs/video-assets` in their numbered order.

---

Create a polished 80-second, 16:9 English product explainer video for **Vartola**, a UAE productive-asset financing and tokenization platform. Deliver a final MP4 suitable for an investor meeting, accelerator application, and website hero section.

## Mandatory visual direction

- Premium fintech and real-world-assets aesthetic.
- Main palette: deep near-black green `#07120F`, forest green `#0D7A52`, bright accent green `#15C77A`, white, and warm off-white `#F6FAF8`.
- Use clean editorial motion graphics, restrained glow, thin connecting lines, animated ledgers, progress bars, wallet movements, and subtle blockchain nodes.
- Keep the interface screenshots authentic. Do not redraw or invent different product screens.
- Crop browser chrome and empty space where appropriate, but never remove or alter essential UI information.
- Animate screenshots with slow push-ins, parallax, cursor highlights, focus frames, and short callout labels.
- Do not display passwords, personal KYC data, API keys, secret keys, wallet private keys, or fabricated regulatory approvals.
- Clearly label the blockchain environment as **Stellar Testnet — no real monetary value**.
- Avoid crypto speculation imagery, coins spinning in space, excessive neon, meme aesthetics, and guaranteed-return language.

## Timeline, narration, and visuals

### Scene 1 — 0:00–0:10 — The financing gap

Narration: “Small businesses do not fail for lack of demand. They stall when the vehicles and equipment needed to grow remain out of reach.”

Visual: Dubai logistics map, delivery routes and real commercial vehicles. Orders accumulate while one missing vehicle space is outlined in green. End with that space becoming a financing request.

On-screen text: `Productive assets unlock SME growth`

### Scene 2 — 0:10–0:20 — SME application

Narration: “Vartola turns a verified asset request into a structured financing opportunity, with the company, asset, risk and repayment terms reviewed first.”

Visual: Use `04-sme-application.png`. Crop tightly around Asset Details, animate the values entering, highlight documents and the Submit Application button. Transition documents into verification ticks.

On-screen text: `Apply · Verify · Structure`

### Scene 3 — 0:20–0:30 — Underwriting

Narration: “Underwriting evaluates the business, documents and productive asset before an approved facility can move into the investment workflow.”

Visual: Use `05-underwriting-review.png`. Animate the completed-review counter, then show a three-part risk assessment: business, asset, deal. Finish on a clear approval stamp and facility record.

On-screen text: `Risk-led underwriting`

### Scene 4 — 0:30–0:40 — Opportunity creation

Narration: “Approved facilities become transparent opportunities showing asset value, target return, term, minimum commitment and the businesses operating the fleet.”

Visual: Start with `06-create-opportunity.png`, then transition to `02-admin-opportunities.png`. Highlight the approved asset being intentionally attached to a pool. Do not imply automatic attachment.

On-screen text: `Real asset · Clear terms · Controlled pool`

### Scene 5 — 0:40–0:50 — Investor decision

Narration: “Investors compare opportunities, review the underlying fleet and commit capital from their wallet while funding progress remains visible in real time.”

Visual: Use `01-investor-marketplace.png`, moving between multiple cards and funding bars. Then use `03-opportunity-details.png` with a gentle pan across Overview, Businesses, Vehicles, Risk, Payment Model and Documents.

On-screen text: `Choose with context`

### Scene 6 — 0:50–1:00 — Funding and deployment

Narration: “When the target is reached, funds are released to the business and converted into a monthly schedule separating principal, income and platform fees.”

Visual: A green value stream moves from several investor wallets into one ring-fenced facility, then toward commercial vehicles. Split a monthly payment into three labelled components.

On-screen text: `Principal · Investor income · Servicing fee`

### Scene 7 — 1:00–1:10 — Repayment and investor returns

Narration: “Each business payment is recorded once, then distributed automatically to investors according to their exact share, with performance visible over time.”

Visual: One SME payment enters the ledger and splits proportionally across investor wallets. Show animated charts for deployed capital, principal returned, net income and outstanding principal.

On-screen text: `One payment · Proportional distribution`

### Scene 8 — 1:10–1:20 — Verifiable evidence

Narration: “Simulation proves the complete lifecycle first. Stellar Testnet then anchors each record with a public transaction hash, preparing Vartola for a regulated production path.”

Visual: Show the internal ledger transforming into a chain of verified records. Display a generic shortened transaction hash and an external-link symbol. Finish with Vartola logo, fleet silhouette and the final call to action.

On-screen text: `Stellar Testnet evidence` followed by `Vartola — Productive asset finance, made transparent.`

## Voice and audio

- Professional English voice, neutral international accent, confident and warm.
- Approximately 145–155 words per minute.
- Use subtle modern corporate music, never overpowering the narration.
- Add restrained interface clicks, soft whooshes and confirmation sounds.
- Duck music underneath every spoken line.

## Captions and text

- Burn in accurate English captions, maximum five words per caption group.
- White captions with a subtle dark outline, inside 16:9 safe margins.
- Use only the exact on-screen phrases specified above.
- Do not invent financial performance, customer numbers, regulatory licences or guaranteed returns.

## Final quality checks

- Exactly 80 seconds, 1920×1080, H.264 MP4, 25 or 30 fps.
- No stretched or distorted screenshots.
- No blank space around screenshots; use deliberate crops and branded backgrounds.
- No unreadable generated UI text.
- Narration, captions and scene changes must stay synchronized.
- End card must remain visible for at least 2.5 seconds.

Return the final MP4 plus a clean version without captions if the platform supports multiple outputs.
