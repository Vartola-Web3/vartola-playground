# SME pilot letter of intent (template)

> **Non-binding.** Subject to legal review. Subject to the regulatory structure being confirmed. No commitment to fund or to provide services. No regulated activity has been initiated. Vartola operates a Working Alpha on Stellar Testnet only, with no real money.

## Purpose

Records that an SME is interested in taking part in a supervised Testnet pilot of Vartola productive-asset finance. It is a statement of interest, not a financing commitment.

## What the SME states

- Legal name, trade licence number and contact
- Asset type and approximate quantity it would like to finance
- Indicative asset value and intended use
- Willingness to share business documents for test underwriting

## What the pilot is

- Testnet only: no real money moves and VTAED has no value
- Test underwriting and a Testnet facility to show the lifecycle
- Feedback on the product and servicing workflow

## What it is not

- No loan, lease or financing offer
- No obligation on either party
- No exclusivity

## Data handling

- Documents stay in private storage
- Personal and commercial data stay off-chain; only hashes may be anchored

## Signatures

Only if the parties later agree a binding document, drafted by counsel. This template creates no obligation.
