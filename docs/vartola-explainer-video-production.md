# Vartola — 80-second English Explainer

Status: production-ready script and shot plan. Final AI rendering requires an enabled media-generation plan; the attempted render was rejected before spending credits.

## Locked direction

- Format: 16:9 faceless explainer, 80 seconds, eight ten-second blocks
- Look: warm cream editorial collage, monochrome halftone cutouts, forest-green accent only
- Voice: Remy, consistent English narrator
- Captions: bold, burned-in English captions from the authored script
- Through-line: a green capital token moves from investor wallet to productive asset, then returns as principal and income

## Narration and scenes

1. **“Small businesses do not fail for lack of demand. They stall when the vehicles and equipment needed to grow remain out of reach.”**
   A delivery route appears, an empty vehicle space stamps down, and waiting orders stack behind it.

2. **“Vartola turns a verified asset request into a structured financing opportunity, with the company, asset, risk and repayment terms reviewed first.”**
   Company, vehicle, documents and risk markers arrive in sequence and lock into one approved file.

3. **“Approved assets enter a transparent pool where investors can see the yield, term, minimum commitment and the real businesses using the fleet.”**
   Asset cards assemble into a pool while the green token waits beside an investor wallet.

4. **“An investor tops up, chooses an opportunity and commits capital. The platform reserves the amount until the funding target is completely reached.”**
   The token leaves the wallet, fills a dot-matrix funding gauge and clicks into the completed pool.

5. **“Once fully funded, capital is released to the business and converted into a clear monthly schedule separating principal, income and platform servicing fees.”**
   The funded fleet rolls toward the SME while a three-part payment ribbon unfolds beneath it.

6. **“The business pays from its wallet, and every confirmed installment is distributed automatically to investors according to their exact ownership share.”**
   One payment splits proportionally across several investor wallets; no amount is duplicated or lost.

7. **“Investors track deployed capital, principal returned and net income, while operators monitor outstanding debt, collections, fees and late payments in real time.”**
   Two clean dashboard-like data collages rise from ledger entries, using abstract bars and dots without generated text.

8. **“Simulation proves the complete flow first. Stellar Testnet then anchors each record with a public transaction hash, ready for a regulated production path.”**
   The green token becomes a verified chain link, the full lifecycle closes, and the final fleet moves forward.
